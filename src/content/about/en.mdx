The symbol of everlasting change — the sign of the I Ching — represents how change is inherent to life. Being capable of looking at oneself can lead to significant change in the way we deal with ourselves and our lives.

Therapeutic engagement and personal responsibility form the guiding thread. I work from a **client-centered and Gestalt therapeutic** approach, always starting from your own story, your own pace.

I am a licensed clinical psychologist (KU Leuven) and hold a diploma in client-centered and Gestalt therapy from Flanders Management School in Turnhout. I work in **Dutch**, **English**, and occasionally **French**.
